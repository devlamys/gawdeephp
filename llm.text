# Gawdee — Pure Food & Natural Wellness

> Gawdee is an authentic organic food and natural wellness storefront offering traditional, thoughtfully sourced, lab-tested pantry essentials and daily family nutrition.

## About Gawdee
Gawdee produces and delivers traditional, unadulterated food products crafted for modern healthy living. Our core philosophy centers on pure ingredients, ethical sourcing, zero synthetic additives, and traditional preparation methods (such as ancient Bilona hand-churning for ghee and raw, unheated forest honey extraction).

## Storefront Capabilities & Technical Stack
- **Architecture**: PHP 8+ strict-typed web application backed by an embedded SQLite database (`storage/gawdee.sqlite`).
- **AI Assistant**: Built-in Groq / OpenAI AI Studio integration (`/api/chat.php`) providing custom product recommendations, ingredient safety advice, and nutritional guidance.
- **Payment Verification**: Razorpay online payment gateway integration with HMAC-SHA256 signature verification and server-priced checkout.
- **Logistics & Courier**: Integrated DTDC merchant API adapter for automated shipment tracking and dispatch management.
